## 1. SB 379 (2025), 83rd Session — the solar lending and leasing rewrite

**Source:** https://www.leg.state.nv.us/Session/83rd2025/Bills/SB/SB379_EN.pdf
Read 2026-11-03. Also cross-checked against NRS 598.98216.

### Section 23.2 — survey-triggered rescission (used on the survey page)

> "Before or after an agreement for the lease or purchase of a distributed
> generation system or a power purchase agreement has been executed, the solar
> installation company shall perform an on-site physical survey of the premises
> ... and provide the results of the physical survey to the lessee, purchaser or
> host customer."

If the survey performed **after** execution reveals a condition that would
necessitate altering the agreement **to increase the price** or **change the type
of equipment**, the customer may **within 3 business days after receipt of the
survey results** rescind:
- (a) the lease/purchase agreement or the PPA, and
- (b) for a purchaser who financed the purchase, **the loan agreement as well**.

**Subsection 3 — the narrow exception.** Does NOT apply to an equipment
substitution if **all three** are true:
1. the substituted equipment is of equal or superior quality;
2. the substitution does not require the customer to incur any additional cost;
3. it does not reduce estimated first-year production by more than 5 percent.

Notice may be delivered, mailed, telegraphed, **or sent by email** to the address
in the agreement, and must state intent to rescind.

### Section 23.15 / NRS 598.98216 — the three rescission triggers

Three independent windows, all "not later than midnight of the":
- **third business day** after signing — borrower/customer **under 60**;
- **tenth business day** after signing — borrower/customer **60 or older**;
- **third business day after receipt of on-site survey results** (per section 23.2).

### Pre-PTO payment ceiling (used on the payment page)

Appears as item 16 of the required contents of a purchase agreement, and again in
the lease/PPA required-contents list:

> "the purchaser is not required to pay any money to the solar installation
> company, other than an initial down payment or deposit that may be charged in an
> amount that does not exceed **$1,000 or 10 percent of the aggregate contract
> price, whichever is less**, ... until: (a) if the system will be connected to the
> power grid, the system **receives permission from the appropriate public utility
> to connect** to the power grid; (b) if the system will not be connected to the
> power grid, the system **passes a final inspection and becomes operational**."

Cross-checked against **NRS 598.982185**.

### Other confirmed provisions

- **NRS 598.9822** — a *knowing* failure to comply with the required contents
  makes the agreement **voidable** by the purchaser. Failure to comply is a
  deceptive trade practice.
- **NRS 598.98213** — recorded verbal confirmation required, at execution or within
  48 hours, and installation must not begin until the recording exists; keep 5 years
  after final inspection.
- **NRS 598.9809** — cover-page rescission notice in at least 16-point type.
- **NRS 624.875** — a non-compliant residential PV contract is voidable by the
  owner; required contents include the contractor's licence number **and the
  monetary limit on that licence**.
- **NRS 624.331(1)** — complaint against a licensee must be filed within 4 years.

## 2. NV Energy — Net Metering FAQs

**Source:** https://www.nvenergy.com/account-services/energy-pricing-plans/net-metering/net-metering-faqs
Read 2026-11-03. Every tier figure below is quoted from this page.

- **NMR-G** — systems installed **before 31 December 2015**. Grandfathered into
  original rules. Excess energy credited **1:1 in kilowatt-hours**, banked and
  rolled forward. Closed to new customers.
- **NMR-A** — **southern Nevada**, systems installed **1 January 2016 –
  14 June 2017**. Monetary credit for **all** exported energy regardless of
  delivered energy; remaining credits paid by **cheque** at year end.
- **NMR-405** — **25 kW or less**, applied for and/or installed **from
  15 June 2017**. **The default rate for southern Nevada.** Monetary excess-energy
  credit, rolls over monthly. Four tiers:
  - **Tier 1 — 95%** of the consumption charge
  - **Tier 2 — 88%**
  - **Tier 3 — 81%**
  - **Tier 4 — 75%**
  - **"assigned based on when their net metering application was received"**
  - Each tier closed to new customers once **80 MW** of solar capacity was applied
    for; **Tier 4 remains open**.
- **NMR-2025** — **northern Nevada only**, default from **1 October 2025**,
  **15-minute netting**. NV Energy states explicitly it "is not applicable to
  existing northern Nevada net metering customers **or southern Nevada** net
  metering customers."
- **NMR-B** — systems **over 25 kW**.

**Monthly netting (southern Nevada):** within a billing period, exported energy is
netted against delivered energy. Excess remaining earns **75% of the retail
volumetric rate less public policy charges**, placed in a carryover bank. Carryover
cannot be applied to the Basic Service Charge.

**The 20-year lock** at the original installation address is documented by the PUCN
and by Solar United Neighbors; NV Energy confirms the tier structure itself.

**Two sequential 10-day review phases:** after application submission, a 10-day
application review phase, then a second 10-day review period for final
documentation including building permit and inspection card.

**Note:** the commonly repeated claim that 15-minute netting applies to Las Vegas
is wrong — that is northern Nevada (NMR-2025). Southern Nevada stays monthly.

## 3. Nevada Secretary of State — UCC filings

**Source:** https://www.nvsos.gov/sos/businesses/ucc
Read 2026-11-03.

- UCC filings in Nevada are administered **centrally by the Secretary of State**,
  **not** by Clark County. A county-level search can come back clean while a
  financing statement sits in the state registry. (Several other states record
  fixture filings at county level — that is where the confusion comes from.)
- Search mechanism: **UCC11 Information Request**.
- **Online filing fee: $30.** Mail-in UCC11: $60.
- UCC1 Financing Statement online: **$30**.
- Filings available online via SilverFlume (nvsilverflume.gov).
- UCC phone line: 775-684-7100, Mon–Fri 8:00–17:00.
