# Nevada solar-contract facts: verified 3 November 2026

Every fact below was checked against a primary or official source on 3 Nov 2026.
Re-check the dated items before relying on them in copy.

## Statutes (verified against statute text)

- **NRS 598.98216** — a purchaser, lessee or PPA host customer may rescind or cancel
  without penalty by written notice delivered, mailed, telegraphed **or emailed** not
  later than midnight of the **third business day** after signing. Notice may go to the
  email address printed on the required contract cover page.
  Source: https://law.justia.com/codes/nevada/chapter-598/statute-598-98216/

- **NRS 598.982149(1)** — a borrower under a distributed-generation loan may rescind
  within 3 business days; **the tenth business day if the borrower is 60 or older**;
  or 3 business days after receiving on-site survey results under NRS 598.982145.
  The 60+ window is the single clearest differentiator in this market.

- **NRS 598.982148** — a **power purchase agreement** is voidable for **3 years** if the
  installer was unlicensed or was acting outside the monetary limit of its licence at
  signing. SCOPE NOTE (verified 2026-11-03): the statutory title is "Power purchase
  agreement voidable by host customer if solar installation company is not properly
  licensed or acting within monetary limit" — it is written for PPAs. Whether an
  equivalent argument reaches a lease or a financed purchase is a legal question and
  must NOT be stated as settled. The clause originated as SB 440 Sec. 14.4.
  Separately, the financier must verify the installer's licence and limit, failing which
  the loan, lease or PPA is voidable for 3 years. This requires **no proof of fraud** —
  only licence status, which is publicly checkable.

- **NRS 598.9822** — failure to comply with NRS 598.9801–598.9822 is a deceptive trade
  practice; a **knowing** failure (expressly including failure to keep the NRS 598.98213
  recording) renders the agreement **voidable**; and a violation "constitutes consumer
  fraud for the purposes of NRS 41.600" (damages, equitable relief, costs and mandatory
  reasonable attorney's fees).

- **NRS 598.98213** — the installer must make a **recorded** verbal confirmation of
  identity, cover-page terms and the customer's understanding, at execution or within
  48 hours, must not begin installation until the recording exists, and must keep it
  5 years after final inspection.

- **NRS 598.982185** — no payment before the system receives permission to operate or
  passes final inspection, except a down payment of **$1,000 or 10%**, whichever is less.

- **NRS 598.9819 / 598.982183** — any agreement referencing utility electricity prices
  must carry a **12-point** disclaimer that rates may go up or down and savings may vary;
  a financier may not advertise a false or misleading statement about a utility's rate.

- **NRS 598.9809** — cover-page rescission notice in at least **16-point** type plus the
  required disclosure block in at least 10-point type.

- **NRS 624.875** — a non-compliant residential photovoltaic contract is **voidable by
  the owner**. Required contents include the contractor's licence number **and the
  monetary limit on that licence**.

- **NRS 624.331(1)** — a complaint against a licensee must be filed within **4 years**.
  **NRS 624.480** — Recovery Fund complaint within 4 years of completion.

## Enforcement (verified from NSCB releases and press)

- **Titan Solar Power NV** — found guilty on **70 counts** against 29 homeowners, fined
  **$400,000** at a July 2024 hearing it did not attend. ALJ Richard Scotti: Titan
  "caused significant harm, and constitutes a serious threat to the public's health,
  safety, and welfare." Licence suspended 18 June 2024, **revoked 17 July 2024**.
  Ceased operations 13 June 2024. Roughly **2,524 Las Vegas permits**. No successor
  entity, so the workmanship warranty is void while the financing obligation continues.

- **Solarize LLC dba Get Solarize** — 46 counts, **$460,000** fine (reported as the
  statutory maximum), licence revoked 12 June 2024; Frances Kim Le, Michael Sean Hogan
  and Hamid Moradi permanently barred. Homeowner Barbara Erekson documented $21,500 in
  damage from wrong inverters; 13 homeowners identified as harmed.

- **Arcadia Solar NV** — 58 allegations, **$58,000** fine, licence revoked 22 January
  2025, John Francis Murphy banned indefinitely.

- **Sunnova Energy Corporation** — licence 79706 **revoked 6 May 2026** on 16 violations,
  $500 per allegation; Walter Baker and Timothy Mathis permanently banned.

- **Derek Carbajal (Lifetime Power)** — collected nearly **$250,000** from eight
  vulnerable homeowners, most elderly, for work never performed, routinely demanding at
  least half upfront against the legal $1,000-or-10% cap. Sentenced August 2024 to
  12–90 months (suspended), 36 months' probation, **$245,264 restitution**.

## Market scale (verified)

- NSCB receives about **3,000 complaints a year** and has said **nearly one-third**
  concern solar companies. Solar complaints rose from 38 (2021) to 273 (2023).
- **14,435 Nevada homes** (11,091 in Clark County) hold a permit from a failed installer.
- Nevada residential solar permitting fell **56%**, from 20,736 permits (2023) to
  9,160 (2025). Clark County fell 55% to 7,850.
- **Sunrun pulled 2,659 of 12,204** City of Las Vegas residential permits 2023–2025
  (**22%**), the largest single share.
- **Freedom Forever** filed Chapter 11 on 15 Apr 2026 (D. Del. 26-10522) and converted
  to Chapter 7 liquidation on 7 Aug 2026 — 1,802 Las Vegas permits, 96,370 nationally.
- Clark County battery attach rate on 2025 permits: **4.2%** (6.5% statewide).
- The **30% federal residential solar tax credit expired 31 December 2025.**

## The demand charge (dated — re-check before use)

- NV Energy's Southern Nevada **Daily Demand** charge is scheduled for
  **1 January 2027**, after delays from April 2026 and October 2026.
- Upheld by two district courts (Clark County Judge Holthus; Carson City Judge
  Woodbury) in late May 2026. **On appeal to the Nevada Supreme Court with no hearing
  scheduled.** Treat the date as movable and date every page that mentions it.
- Roughly **128,861** Southern Nevada net-metered customers out of 1,064,543 total;
  about 144,000 Nevada homes have solar.
- Net metering: Tier 4 = **75%** of retail, locked 20 years at the install address.
  Tiers 1–3 closed at 95% / 88% / 81%.
- **15-minute netting applies to Northern Nevada, not Southern Nevada.** This is
  widely misunderstood and worth stating explicitly.

## The complaint maze (verified as circular)

- The **AG** FAQ says PUCN handles complaints against solar companies.
- The **PUCN** says it "facilitates" complaints to NSCB, AG BCP, B&I Consumer Fraud
  and the BBB — it routes rather than adjudicates.
- The **NSCB** has its own Solar Investigations Unit and complaint form.
- The AG's own page adds urgency nobody amplifies: *"It is recommended that purchasers
  of a Solar System act quickly to void their contract..."*
- AG BCP review takes **14–45 business days**.
- Recovery Fund: committee meets roughly every **60 days**, pays about **45 days** after
  the order is final, caps at **$40,000**, excludes attorney's fees, and the decision is
  **final and not judicially reviewable** (NRS 624.510(4)).
- NRS 624.895(1)(b) lets the Board refer a violation to the Attorney General as a
  potential deceptive trade practice.
