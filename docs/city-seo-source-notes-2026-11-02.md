# Verified sources for the city-page SEO repair

Google Search Central explicitly states that the meta keywords tag has no effect on Google indexing or ranking. It can still satisfy a third-party scanner. Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/special-tags

Google's AI-search guidance recommends useful original content, crawlable pages, accurate technical structure, reduced duplication, and appropriate structured data. It does not require special AEO/GEO schema and does not guarantee inclusion or rankings. Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide

The FTC distinguishes purchases, loans, leases and PPAs, explains that early termination can be difficult or expensive, and advises reviewing contract duration, increases, warranties, production, transfer and termination terms. It recommends contacting the company and then relevant consumer agencies for unresolved issues. Source: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/solar-power-your-home

The Texas Attorney General complaint page requests business details, transaction dates, contract/payment information and resolution efforts. Complaints are informational; a reference number does not mean a case/investigation exists. The office cannot give individual legal advice. Source: https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/file-consumer-complaint

Arizona DIFI links consumers to the Attorney General Consumer Protection Office for unfair business practices. The old guessed `/consumer/complaint` and `/consumer/solar` paths could not be extracted and are not used as verified sources. Verified route directory: https://difi.az.gov/node/268

Utah's official residential solar retailer FAQ covers purchase/lease/PPA definitions, retailer registration and disclosures, and links to the complaint service. Parent resolved the city review's verification flag using this authoritative page rather than inventing a deadline or remedy. Sources: https://commerce.utah.gov/dcp/residential-solar-panel-retailer/residential-solar-panel-retailer-frequently-asked-questions/ and https://services.commerce.utah.gov/dcp-complaint/
