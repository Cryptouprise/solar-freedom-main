## Blue Raven / SunPower ownership chain — primary sources
- SunPower acquired Blue Raven Solar in **October 2021** for up to **$165 million**
  (PR Newswire, 5 Oct 2021).
- **SunPower filed Chapter 11 on 5 August 2024** and announced a stalking-horse asset purchase
  agreement with **Complete Solaria** to sell **Blue Raven Solar and New Homes for $45 million cash**
  (SunPower press release filed with the SEC, 5 Aug 2024).
- The sale was approved by the bankruptcy court (Reuters, 29 Aug 2024; docket approval 23 Sep 2024).
- The brand now trades as SunPower Inc. (formerly Complete Solaria).
