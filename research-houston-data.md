# Houston/Texas Solar Research for Infographic Images

## The Hidden Demand Fee / TDU Charge Reality

### What solar reps told homeowners:
- "Your solar will cover your entire electric bill"
- "You'll pay $0 to the utility company"
- "One payment replaces your electric bill"

### The reality in Houston (CenterPoint Energy service area):
- **CenterPoint TDU delivery rate**: 6.001 cents/kWh (among highest in Texas) + $4.90/month base charge
- **Total TDU charges for 1,000 kWh home**: $40-55/month — THIS NEVER GOES AWAY even with solar
- **TDU charges = 25-40% of total electric bill** — solar doesn't eliminate this
- **Energy rate from retail provider**: 8-14 cents/kWh on top of TDU
- **Net result**: Homeowner pays solar payment ($150-250/mo) PLUS TDU delivery ($40-55/mo) PLUS any excess usage

### The Lie Visualized:
- What they were told: Solar payment = $180/mo, Electric bill = $0
- Reality: Solar payment = $180/mo + TDU fee = $45/mo + excess usage = $20/mo = $245/mo TOTAL
- Before solar: Electric bill was $180-220/mo
- **Net loss: $25-65/month MORE than before solar**

1. **GoodLeap** — BBB page has 100+ pages of complaints (1000+ total complaints). Roseville CA based. Common issues: hidden dealer fees (20-30% of loan), balloon payments at year 18, tax credit not applied correctly

## Key Legal Frameworks for Texas
- **Texas DTPA** (Deceptive Trade Practices Act) — Business & Commerce Code § 17.46
- **Remedies**: Actual damages, up to 3x damages for knowing violations, attorney fees
- **FTC Cooling-Off Rule**: 3 business days to cancel door-to-door contracts
- **TILA (Truth in Lending Act)**: Federal — requires full disclosure of all loan terms
- **FTC Holder Rule**: Allows claims against lenders (GoodLeap/Mosaic) for installer's misconduct
