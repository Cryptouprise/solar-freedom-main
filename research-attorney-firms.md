## KEY PATTERNS IDENTIFIED

### Pattern 1: The Money Is in Going After LENDERS, Not Installers
- Installers go bankrupt (Pink Energy, SunPower, Momentum Solar, etc.)
- Lenders (GoodLeap, Mosaic, Tech CU, Solarity) have money and are liable under FTC Holder Rule
- Kneupper & Covey, Prevost, KBK all target lenders specifically
- **Implication for us:** Leads involving LOAN disputes (not leases) are more valuable because attorneys can actually recover money

### Pattern 2: Arbitration Is the Battlefield
- 80-90% of cases settle before arbitration hearing
- Arbitration awards range from $30K-$100K+ (loan cancellation + damages)
- Lenders prefer to settle quietly to avoid setting legal precedent
- **Implication for us:** Leads with documented evidence (contracts, emails, recordings) are more valuable

### Pattern 3: TILA + FTC Holder Rule = The Winning Combo
- Almost every successful firm uses this same legal theory
- TILA violations (undisclosed dealer fees, wrong APR, missing disclosures) → 3-year rescission window
- FTC Holder Rule → lender liable for installer's fraud
- **Implication for us:** Our content should educate homeowners on these specific legal theories to pre-qualify leads

### Pattern 4: Timeshare Exit → Solar Exit Pipeline
- Stonegate Firm pivoted from timeshare to solar
- Same business model: flat fee exit service with attorney backing
- **Implication for us:** There may be other timeshare exit companies entering solar. They're potential lead buyers.

### Pattern 5: Marketing Firms as Middlemen
- SCRC, Solar Equity Solutions, Stonegate are all MARKETING companies that work with attorneys
- They charge flat fees ($3K-$10K estimated) and refer to partner attorneys
- **Implication for us:** We can either compete with them OR sell leads to them. They're already buying leads somewhere.

### Pattern 6: Geographic Concentration
- CA: KBK Legal dominates (Home Solicitation Sales Act is uniquely powerful in CA)
- TX: Bennett Legal dominates (DTPA + large market)
- FL: Vargas Gonzalez dominates (class actions)
- Nationwide: Kneupper & Covey, Prevost, Flitter Milz
- **Implication for us:** Our city pages should match to the right firms per geography

### Pattern 7: Loan vs Lease Distinction Matters
- Prevost explicitly won't take lease cases
- Loan cases have stronger legal theories (TILA, Holder Rule)
- Lease cases rely more on state consumer protection (UDAP/DTPA)
- **Implication for us:** Qualifying leads by contract type (loan vs lease) increases their value
